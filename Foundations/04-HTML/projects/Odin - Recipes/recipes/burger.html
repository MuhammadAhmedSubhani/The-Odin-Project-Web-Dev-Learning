Burger

Description

A burger is a popular fast-food dish made with a juicy, seasoned beef patty placed inside a soft bun. It is topped with fresh vegetables, cheese, and delicious sauces to create a tasty and satisfying meal.

Ingredients

  • 1 burger bun
  • 1 beef patty
  • 1 slice of cheese
  • 2 lettuce leaves

Steps

  1. Prepare the patty: Season the beef patty with salt and black pepper.
  2. Cook the patty: Heat oil in a pan and cook the patty until fully cooked.
  3. Toast the bun: Cut the burger bun into two halves and lightly toast them.
  4. Add sauces: Spread mayonnaise and ketchup on the inside of both bun halves.
