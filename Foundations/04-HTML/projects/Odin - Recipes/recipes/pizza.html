Pizza

Pizza is a popular Italian dish made with a round, flat bread base topped with pizza sauce, cheese, vegetables, and chicken. It is baked until the crust becomes golden and the cheese melts, creating a delicious and flavorful meal.

Ingredients

  • 1 pizza dough
  • ½ cup pizza sauce
  • 1 cup mozzarella cheese
  • ½ cup cooked chicken

Steps

  1. Prepare the dough: Roll the pizza dough into a round shape.
  2. Add pizza sauce: Spread pizza sauce evenly over the dough.
  3. Add cheese: Sprinkle mozzarella cheese over the sauce.
  4. Add toppings: Spread cooked chicken, capsicum, onion, tomato, and olives over the cheese.
